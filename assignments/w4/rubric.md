# Technical Report Rubric

## Objective Statement / (15 points)

- **Beginning (~50%):** Response does not relate to the purpose, knowledge gap, and analysis performed in the report.
- **Developing (~75%):** The purpose, knowledge gap, OR analysis is missing. Unneeded details or material that belongs in other sections is included.
- **Accomplished (~90%):** The purpose, knowledge gap, and analysis are clear, but it may be slightly unclear how they are connected OR include some unnecessary detail about the broader issue or methods.
- **Exemplary (100%):** The reason for conducting the analysis is explained concisely. The specific product that will be provided to address the issue is stated. The specific statistical analysis that was performed to addresses the question or knowledge gap is given.

## Methods / (25 points)

- **Beginning (~50%):** The methods do not provide even a basic outline of how the calculations were performed. Equations are handwritten and may have errors in symbology, numbering, and variable definition. Excel function names are not given consistently.
- **Developing (~75%):** The methods lack key components needed for replication or include substantial unnecessary detail, but generally outline the procedure used. Reasoning is not given for why the methods were used. Minor issues may exist with equation formatting.
- **Accomplished (~90%):** The methods may lack one or two key minor details or include one or two unnecessary details. It is clear why the methods were used and how they address the objective. Numbered equations use proper symbology with variables defined in the text. Correct function names are given for Excel functions.
- **Exemplary (100%):** The source, records, and variables for the dataset are given. Methods are described in sufficient detail that they could be replicated without unnecessary detail. It is clear why the methods were used and how they address the objective. Numbered equations use proper symbology with variables defined in the text. Correct function names are given for Excel functions.

## Results and Conclusions / (25 points)

- **Beginning (~50%):** The results and conclusions do not follow the CER framework. Support for the claim may be based on factors outside the statistical analysis.
- **Developing (~75%):** An attempt is made to use the claim, evidence, and reasoning framework, but it is lacking in 3 or more of the qualities that characterize an Exemplary response. Support for the claim may be based on factors outside the statistical analysis.
- **Accomplished (~90%):** The claim is clear and specific and is based on a correct interpretation of the results. The evidence and reasoning may have one or two deficiencies in providing specifics, interpreting material, or tying together CER components.
- **Exemplary (100%):** The claim is clear and specific and is based on a correct interpretation of the results. The evidence is a specific, accurate and objective description of the results. The reasoning directly connects the claim to the evidence and provides the statistical logic behind the connection.

## Calculations / (25 points)

- **Beginning (~50%):** The analysis method is not appropriate for the objective. Frequent errors are present in calculations or wrong statistics are calculated. Excel file not provided.
- **Developing (~75%):** The analysis method may not be appropriate for the objective. Occasional errors are present in calculations. Work in Excel may not match results in report.
- **Accomplished (~90%):** An appropriate analysis method is selected. Calculations may have 1-2 minor errors. Work in Excel matches results in report.
- **Exemplary (100%):** An appropriate analysis method is selected. Calculations are correct. Work in Excel matches results in report.

## Figures if relevant / (Points included with Calculations)

- **Beginning (~50%):** The figure may be hand drawn and have significant errors in the values displayed or use the incorrect plot type. It lacks the Anatomy of a Good Figure.
- **Developing (~75%):** The figure may be hand drawn and have minor errors in the values displayed but uses the correct plot type. It lacks the Anatomy of a Good Figure. Histogram has too many or too few bins to show distribution of the data.
- **Accomplished (~90%):** The figure is computer generated, uses the correct plot type, and has the correct values shown. It may lack 1 or 2 aspects of the Anatomy of a Good Figure. Histogram has an appropriate number of bins.
- **Exemplary (100%):** The figure is computer generated, uses the correct plot type, and has the correct values shown. It follows the Anatomy of a Good Figure. Histograms have an appropriate number of bins to show distribution of the data.

## Tables if relevant / (Points included with Calculations)

- **Beginning (~50%):** The results are not presented in a table.
- **Developing (~75%):** Results may be in a neat, hand draw table, or in computer-generated form with deficiencies in neatness and labeling.
- **Accomplished (~90%):** Tables are computer-generated but may have 1-2 deficiencies in neatness and labeling.
- **Exemplary (100%):** Tables are neat and computer-generated. Values are clearly labeled and include units if relevant.

## Full Assignment / (10 points)

- **Beginning (~50%):** Writing is not in complete sentences. Errors present to the point the document is unreadable. Some components may only be in Excel file. Appropriate section headings not used.
- **Developing (~75%):** Writing is in complete sentences but may be in bullet points and does not use past tense. Many errors in grammar and spelling. Some components may only be in Excel file. Appropriate section headings not used.
- **Accomplished (~90%):** Writing is in complete sentences in paragraph form and mostly uses past tense. Occasional errors in grammar and spelling. All required components present in Word or pdf file. Appropriate section headings used.
- **Exemplary (100%):** Writing is in complete sentences in paragraph form and uses the past tense. Correct grammar and spelling used throughout. All required components present in Word or pdf file. Appropriate section headings used.
